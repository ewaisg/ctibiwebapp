# Manpower & Utilization Guide

Understand staffing levels, capacity, and billable ratios to make informed resourcing decisions.

[!NOTE]
If you don’t see Manpower/Utilization in the sidebar, you may not have permission. Contact an administrator.

## 1. Overview

This module shows who is assigned, available, and how much time is billable vs non-billable. Use it to plan staffing and optimize workloads.

### Who uses it
- Project Managers: plan teams and monitor utilization
- Finance: review billable ratios for revenue planning
- Administrators: oversee org-wide capacity

## 2. Filters & Views

- Date range: focus on a specific window
- Department/Division: narrow to org segments
- Role/Title: analyze by position
- Project: inspect utilization tied to specific projects

## 3. Key Metrics

- Utilization Rate: billable hours ÷ total hours
- Availability: unassigned capacity within timeframe
- Allocation: assigned hours vs planned hours
- Over/Under Utilized: thresholds indicating overload or idle time

[!TIP]
Hover or click on charts to see exact values and underlying data.

## 4. Drilldowns

- Click widgets to view detailed lists of employees, allocations, and hours
- Jump to Projects or Timesheets for context or corrections

## 5. Interpreting Trends

- Rising utilization: potential revenue increase; watch for burnout
- Falling utilization: potential idle capacity; consider reassignments
- Persistent over-utilization: rebalance workloads or increase staffing

## 6. Staffing Workflows

- Adjust assignments:
  1. Open the relevant project
  2. Update team assignments and dates
  3. Confirm role and rates align with services/contracts

- Balance capacity:
  1. Identify under-utilized staff
  2. Reassign or expand project scope

## 7. Troubleshooting

- Missing data: check timesheet approvals and assignment dates
- Odd ratios: ensure services and hours are categorized correctly
- No availability shown: review filters and role settings

## 8. FAQs

- What is a good utilization rate?
  - It varies by org policy; commonly 70–85% for balanced teams.

- Why do dashboards differ from timesheets?
  - Dashboards reflect approved and categorized entries; pending items are excluded.

## 9. Best Practices

- Keep assignments up-to-date with start/end dates
- Review utilization weekly to catch trends early
- Coordinate changes with project managers and finance
