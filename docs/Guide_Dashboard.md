# Dashboard Guide

Understand your KPIs at a glance and drill into details when needed.

[!NOTE]
If you don’t see the Dashboard after sign-in, use the sidebar or ⌘K to navigate.

## 1. Overview

The dashboard aggregates key metrics for utilization, manpower, invoicing status, and recent activity. Use filters to focus and drilldowns to investigate.

## 2. Filters

- Date range: set the period displayed
- Department/Division: limit to organizational segments
- Project: focus on specific projects
- Role: filter by staff roles where applicable

[!TIP]
Filters persist during your session; reset to view the full picture.

## 3. Widgets & KPIs

- Utilization: billable vs total hours
- Manpower: assignments and availability
- Financials: invoice totals, paid vs outstanding
- Recent activity: latest uploads, approvals, and changes

### KPI definitions
- Utilization rate = billable hours ÷ total hours
- Outstanding amount = invoiced total − received payments

## 4. Drilldowns

- Click any widget to view details (lists, tables, or charts)
- From drilldown views, navigate to Projects, Timesheets, or Invoicing for actions

## 5. Navigation Shortcuts

- ⌘K opens the command palette
- Use breadcrumb and sidebar to switch modules quickly

## 6. Saved Views (if enabled)

- Save common filter sets for quick access
- Rename or delete saved views as your needs change

## 7. Troubleshooting

- Data seems stale: refresh; ensure approvals or updates are completed
- Widgets missing: check permissions and filters
- Numbers don’t match reports: align date ranges and approval status

## 8. FAQs

- Why don’t my hours appear?
  - Only approved entries are included.

- Can I export from the dashboard?
  - Use drilldown views or related modules for exports.

## 9. Best Practices

- Review the dashboard at the start/end of each week
- Use filters to compare teams or projects
- Investigate anomalies via drilldowns promptly
