# FAQ & Troubleshooting

Common questions and solutions for end users.

## 1. Access & Permissions

- I can’t access a module
  - You may not have permission. Contact an administrator.

- My role changed but access didn’t
  - Sign out and back in to refresh tokens.

## 2. Data Updates

- My data isn’t updating
  - Refresh. Some dashboards reflect changes after approvals.

- Timesheet approvals not reflected
  - Ensure entries were approved in the correct period.

## 3. Uploads & Imports

- Timesheet upload failed
  - Use the latest template; fix validation errors and re-upload.

- Many rows invalid
  - Check column names, date formats, numeric fields, and codes.

## 4. Invoicing & Rates

- Invoice totals seem off
  - Confirm timesheets approved; verify services and rate templates.

- Partial payments not matching
  - Validate payment amounts and dates; correct entries if needed.

## 5. PDFs

- PDF won’t open
  - Refresh; if it persists, download and open locally.

- Can’t print
  - Printing may be restricted. Download and print if allowed.

## 6. Diagnostics

- Include when reporting issues:
  - Page URL
  - Date/time
  - Steps taken
  - Screenshots (if possible)

## 7. Escalation

- For persistent issues, contact your administrator or support channel with diagnostics.

## 8. Best Practices

- Use the latest templates
- Keep roles/permissions aligned with responsibilities
- Validate data before uploads
- Review dashboards weekly to catch anomalies
